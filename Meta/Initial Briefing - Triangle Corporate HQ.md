## 1. Arrival at Headquarters

### Read Aloud

It might be your first day, or it might be your twenty-fifth anniversary at Triangle Corporate HQ. Either way, it's a day of new beginnings.

Recently, you became a Resonant. An Anomaly took a liking to you, and you were given a choice: take on additional work for the Agency or be sent to the Vault. You chose the former.

Now, you're walking through the lobby of HQ, either having moved here permanently or having been deployed from elsewhere in the massive corporation. Regardless, you're surrounded by a sea of unfamiliar, dour faces as a no-nonsense security guard escorts you through the building. Their uniform looks a little too tight for their bulging physique.

You're eventually led into a beige conference room with plush red carpet and red chairs. A monitor plays a training video without sound. A clock ticks loudly on the wall.

> [!info] GM Notes  
> As each player enters, ask them to describe:
> 
> - What their character looks like.
>     
> - How they enter the room.
>     
> - Whether they do anything notable on their way in.
>     
> 
> Give each player a moment to establish their character before moving on.
> 
> Once everyone has arrived, read the following.

## 2. Meeting the General Manager

### Read Aloud

After a moment that's not long enough to relax, but not short enough to avoid awkwardness, a portly man walks into the room. He wears an ill-fitting suit jacket, a red tie, and a red watch.

"I'm sorry, just one minute. I need to... there, got it."

He sets his briefcase on the table. It clicks once, then again, before popping open.

He pulls out a few things and places a piece of paper in front of himself, along with a stack of papers beside it. He continues unpacking, producing four large red boxes and a number of smaller objects.

A playing card slips out of the briefcase and falls to the floor. He doesn't seem to notice.

### The Playing Card

> [!example] Read Aloud  
> The Jack of Hearts. Except the face is all wrong. It looks like it's screaming.

> [!info] GM Notes  
> If a player examines the card, read the description above. Allow the players to react, but don't force the moment if nobody notices it.

### Bud's Introduction

Once he's finished setting up, the man clears his throat.

"I'm sorry about all that. I'm in a bit of a rush, and I just need to make sure I get this right."

He places a rather large machine in the centre of the table. Lights blink across its surface, and a large screen displays the words:

**PRESENT EMPLOYEE IDENTIFICATION**

"Pay that no mind for now, but it will be important later. Now then, folks, we should get started. My name is Bud, and I'll be your General Manager. I'm supposed to read out this, uh, script to you, so I'll do my best."

## 3. Terms and Conditions

### Bud's Script

> Welcome, new agents, to your first day as Trainees in the program. Before we get started, I want to make sure you've all read and signed the terms and conditions provided to you. Once you have, I can distribute your welcome packs, and we can proceed to sign you in to the Agency Systems.

> [!info] GM Notes  
> Fumble a little with the terms and conditions. Pass out pens as needed, and allow the players to read and sign the paperwork.
> 
> Once everyone has signed, hand out the red boxes containing the welcome packs and continue.

## 4. Welcome Packs and Introductions

### Bud's Script

> Thank you. Inside your welcome pack, you'll find a lanyard, a temporary ID badge, a set of Triangle Agency Dice, and a handy QuickStart Field Guide. Feel free to read the guide at your leisure, but we recommend keeping a copy on hand at all times. Your temporary ID will need to be initialised and linked to your employee ID at this time.

### Read Aloud

Bud looks down at his paperwork, then back at the group.

"Oh, I need to do that. Uh, while I'm entering your information into the system, why don't you introduce yourselves and tell us your favourite cheese... or other thing."

### GM Notes: Character Introductions and Reality

Ask each player to introduce their character.

While Bud enters their information, use the introductions as an opportunity to establish each character's Reality. Flash back to their morning commute, or play out a brief scene involving one of their relationships. This could be their strongest relationship or their weakest.

The questions Bud asks should correspond to the questions the players need to answer as part of their Reality. Each player should answer all three questions in the matrix, but rather than simply asking them to provide answers, play out a scene in which those answers emerge naturally.

Keep the scenes brief enough that everyone gets a turn.

## 5. Life at the Agency

### Bud's Script

> Well, each of you is truly a unique individual, aren't you? Well, I'll be your General Manager while you're all working for the Agency. That is, until you've advanced far enough in the company to no longer need me.
> 
> I hope you'll find your time here a pleasant one. At least more pleasant than the alternative.
> 
> If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to ask. I'm here for you, after all, though most things should be answered by your Field Guide.

### GM Notes: Employee Systems and Game Mechanics

Once Bud has finished his introduction, proceed with registering each agent in the Agency Systems.

Explain that agents must access their own employee portal from their personal devices. Accessing another employee's portal will result in demerits for both the employee being accessed and the person attempting to access it.

Continue explaining the Agency's systems and policies, including:

- Demerits and commendations, including their associated rewards and consequences.
    
- The Agency's life insurance policy.
    
- The regular briefcase and its intended use.
    
- The Ripple Gun and its intended use.
    
- Any other game mechanics the players need to understand before their first assignment.
    

Keep Bud in character throughout. He should be attempting to explain everything in a professional manner, even when the material is uncomfortable, absurd, or difficult to understand.

Give the players time to ask questions and familiarise themselves with their equipment. Once everyone is comfortable, move on to the first assignment.

## 6. The First Assignment

### Read Aloud

"Well, uh, now that everyone's comfortable, I have your first assignment here..."

Bud looks down at the paperwork.

"Uh, oh. A murder. No, wait. Three murders."

He pauses, scanning the page.

"Looks like a number of people out in, uh, Caddy Corner have been turning up dead in a rather unusual way. I'm sorry, I didn't think your first go at this would be so gruesome. Oh God, there's pictures here too..."

He shuffles the papers together and puts them aside.

"Perhaps I'll, uh, keep those. No sense in you having to experience that kind of trauma."

He clears his throat and looks back at the group.

"Anywho, the boffins down in the labs have detected a high concentration of anomalous energies emanating from the area. As field agents, it's your responsibility to investigate, determine whether the origin is an Anomaly, and neutralise the threat if it is, thus stabilising Reality.

"Ideally, we, that is, uh, the Agency, would prefer it if you captured the Anomaly using the regular briefcase. But if it comes to it, you may, of course, rely on the Ripple Gun. Just be sure not to aim the device at any Mundanes or your fellow agents.

"Also, to be clear, it is of the utmost importance that no Mundanes observe, interact with, or otherwise become influenced by any anomalous behaviour. Such exposure is likely to result in the creation of further Anomalies.

"It will be up to your discretion how you contain these loose ends, though you can ask the Agency to step in and send a 'clean-up' team."

## 7. Questions About Caddy Corner

### GM Notes: Bud's Knowledge

Allow the players to ask questions about Caddy Corner. Bud can provide the following information.

#### The Victims

Bud knows the names of all three victims:

- Billie Lee
    
- Tony Jackson
    
- Manny Navarro
    

He also knows that Billie Lee was the first to be killed, but her body was only recently discovered.

#### The Local Area

Bud knows a little about the local nightlife, particularly a venue called 2 Jacks.

He also knows some of the area's history, including the overpass that was supposed to lead somewhere and help make the area into something more, but was never completed.

#### The Abandoned Overpass

Bud knows that construction was abruptly cancelled because of a separate Anomaly. That Anomaly has since been contained, but he doesn't know many of the details.

#### The Agency's Limitations

Bud reminds the agents that, while the Agency can do a great deal, it cannot change anything that has already been established.

He also reminds them that they are not federal or government agents. Impersonating an officer is a crime.

> [!info] GM Notes  
> Allow the players to ask any further questions they have. Bud should answer what he knows, but he shouldn't have information about the current investigation beyond what has been provided to him.

## 8. Employee Registration and the Glitch

> [!danger] Important: The Agents Must Leave the Room  
> Once the briefing is complete, Bud will have each agent log in to the Agency Systems.
> 
> **Make sure the agents actually leave the room.**
> 
> Have each agent leave individually, one after another, until everyone has left. This physical separation is essential to the sequence that follows.
> 
> When the glitch occurs, Bud must not acknowledge it. He should behave as though he saw nothing happen and carry on with his work as usual.
> 
> Do not have Bud explain, investigate, or draw attention to the event. The contrast between the glitch and his complete indifference is the point.

Once everyone has been reassembled in the conference room, the agents can prepare to leave for their first assignment.

## 9. Departure for Caddy Corner

Caddy Corner is approximately an hour's drive from Triangle Corporate HQ.

Once the players are ready, let them make their way there and begin the investigation.
